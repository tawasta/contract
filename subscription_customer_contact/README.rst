=============================
Subscription Customer Contact
=============================

This module extends **Subscriptions** by adding a new field *Customer Contact*
(``customer_contact_id``) on the subscription. When an invoice is generated
from the subscription, the selected contact is propagated into the invoice’s
``customer_contact_id`` field.
